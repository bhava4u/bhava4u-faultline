Risk theme

Critical minerals

Competition for the minerals behind batteries, electronics and defence is reshaping politics in producing countries.

Cobalt, lithium, copper, nickel and rare earths are essential to electric vehicles, electronics and weapons. Supply is concentrated in a handful of countries, processing is dominated by China, and producers increasingly want more of the value at home. That combination brings conflict risk, resource nationalism and great-power competition over who gets access.

What drives the risk

Conflict in producing regions. Eastern DR Congo, home to much of the world’s cobalt and coltan, is at war.

Resource nationalism. Export bans, new royalties and state control are spreading from Africa to Latin America and Southeast Asia.

Great-power competition. The US, China and Europe are competing for deposits from Greenland to the Andes.
